STAATSKOERANT, 11 JULIE 2003 No. 25206 3
PROCLAMATION
by the
President of the Republic of South Africa
No. R. 53, 2003
COMMENCEMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE REPUBLIC OF SOUTH
AFRICA SECOND AMENDMENT ACT, 2003 (ACT NO. 3 OF 2003)
Under section 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa Second Amendment Act,
2003 (Act No. 3 of 2003). I hereby fix 11 July 2003 as the date on which the said Act shall come
into operation.
Given under my Hand and the Seal of the Republic of South Africa at Pretoria this eighth day of
July Two thousand and three.
T. M. MBEKI
President
By Order of the President-in-Cabinet:
P. M. MADUNA
Minister of the Cabinet
