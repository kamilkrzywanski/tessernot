Dear Mr. President:
I write to you regarding a disturbing abuse of power within the Department of the Army.
This week, the Army brought charges of murder against MAJ Matthew Golsteyn, a decorated
Green Beret, even though previous investigations found that charges were unwarranted.
While I cannot begin to understand the motivations of the Army officers that brought this
case, I can speak for MAJ Golsteyn’s character. He is not a murderer. He is an elite warrior that
was executing the mission he was trained to do. He engaged an Afghan bombmaker, who had
built the bomb that was responsible for the death of two Marines only days prior.
As a Marine who served in Iraq and Afghanistan, I respect our Rules of Engagement. I
also recognize the necessity for our men and women in uniform to have the ability to protect
themselves and those they serve with—especially when those threats have already caused a loss
of American lives.
Rather than spending years investigating and reinvestigating the actions of a decorated
Green Beret who seized an opportunity to ensure other Americans were not taken by a Taliban
bomb maker, I believe the Army would be best served by focusing its efforts elsewhere.
Mr. President, I believe the record will show MAJ Golsteyn’s actions were lawful. The
Army has, again, failed one of its most dedicated and faithful warriors, which is why I ask you to
give your discerning attention to this case.
Sincerely,
Duncan Hunter
