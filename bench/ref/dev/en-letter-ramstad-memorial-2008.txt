Dear Friends:
Congratulations on breaking ground for the Eden Prairie Veterans Memorial!
I sincerely regret that I can’t be there in person with you on this historic day, however Congress
is in session and I must be in Washington.
I want to thank from the bottom of my heart all the veterans organizations, community officials
and wonderful people and organizations who have made this day possible by making generous
contributions in honor of our brave veterans.
My special thanks go out to the members of the Memorial Committee for your leadership and
hard work.  What a great addition to Eden Prairie, and what a powerful message for people of all
ages to hear and see, not just on Veterans Day, but each and every day of the year.
Congratulations again and thanks for all your hard work and contributions toward this important
memorial to our brave veterans.  Thank you all for creating this poignant reminder of the true
cost of the freedoms we all enjoy today – and thank you veterans for all you do!
Sincerely,
JIM RAMSTAD
