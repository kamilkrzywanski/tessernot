Boston, Mass.
Subject: Recommendation for rerating of G. H. Sias, leading-
man machinist, and T. C. Farwell and C. C. Sargent, ma-
chinists, to assistant planners and estimators, Machinery
division.
References: (a) Eng.Off's letter IEB:W;R, May 11, 1918.
(b) Comdt's 1st end. No.461, May 14, 1918.
1. The Department disapproves of the recommendations
contained in the above references, as it is believed that the
pay of these employees is adequate for the character of work
performed, especially in view of the fact that all yards are
working overtime, and the schedule rate of pay is, therefore,
only about 75 percent of what the employees actually receive.
