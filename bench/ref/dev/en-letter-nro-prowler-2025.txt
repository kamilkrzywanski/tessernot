This is in response to your 20 February 2025 Freedom of Information
Act (FOIA) request, originally submitted by you to the Defense
Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA), for, "I am interested in any
information related to the payload of shuttle mission STS-38, more
specifically the satellite known publicly as "prowler", "2000-653A", or
"UI.139" also referenced in the following article:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prowler (satellite)". This case was received
in the National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) by way of referral on 18 March
2025.
We have completed a thorough review of your request and have
determined that in accordance with Executive Order 13526 § 3.6(a), the NRO
can neither confirm nor deny the existence or nonexistence of records
responsive to your request. The fact of the existence or nonexistence of
such records is itself currently and properly classified; accordingly, your
request is denied pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 552 (b)(1).
You have the right to appeal this determination to the NRO Appellate
Authority, 14675 Lee Road, Chantilly, VA 20151-1715, within 90 days of the
above date. You may also submit an appeal electronically through the
National FOIA Portal at www.foia.gov or via email to FOIA@nro.mil. Please
include an explanation of the reason(s) for your appeal as part of your
submission. The FOIA also provides that you may seek dispute resolution for
any adverse determination through the NRO FOIA Public Liaison and/or through
the Office of Government Information Services (OGIS). Please refer to the
OGIS public web page at www.ogis.archive.gov for additional information.
