July 18, 1919.
The Honorable
The Governor of Nebraska,
Lincoln, Nebr.
Sir:
In reply to your letter of July 14th, I have
the honor to advise you that the certified copy of the
Joint Resolution Proposing an amendment to the Consti-
tution extending the right of suffrage to women, enclos-
ed in my letter of June 12,1919, is the official form
which was sent to the Governor of each State for submis-
sion to the State Legislature for action thereon.
I have the honor to be, Sir,
Your obedient servant,
FRANK L. POLK.
Acting Secretary of State.
