Subject: Ivey Steel Subcontract
The structural refurbishment/modification of the Umbilical Tower and
Mobile Service Tower at Complex 41 has come to a close. This effort has
been a challenge for all involved. Many factors contributed to the
successful completion of this work effort. The refurbishment phase
depended heavily on cooperation from Ivey Steel field personnel in
detecting deteriorated structure not found by the initial engineering
survey and assisting in resolving workable repairs. The modification
design package had numerous hidden problems that were discovered in
advance, reducing the cost and schedule impact. Ivey Steel can be
credited with many of these finds. Ivey's personnel consciously monitored
the job for these types of errors and worked closely with Martin Marietta
Corporation to resolve them efficiently. In particular, the following
personnel should receive special acknowledgment:
Richard Walls - Site Supervisor
Reynsol Chiles - Site Foreman
Jim MacLaren - Assistant Supervisor
Alfred Stevens - Field Liaison
Martin Marietta Corporation Engineering wishes to express our thanks for
their assistance and we look forward to working with them in the future.
Ben Dusenbery
Mechanical Systems Engineer
Morris S. Worland, Chief, Design,
Construction and Procedures
