Treasury Department,
Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
Washington, D. C. July 23, 1915.
Received from the Honorable John Skelton Williams, Comptroller
of the Currency, Federal Reserve Notes to the Value of Four Hundred
and Forty-two Million, Three Hundred and Forty Thousand Dollars,
placed in my custody in a vault in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing,
and subject to his requisition for shipment to the several Federal Reserve
Banks, in accordance with the instructions of June 24, 1915, from the
Secretary of the Treasury.
$ 442,340,000.
Director of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
