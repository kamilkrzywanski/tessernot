February 3, 1961
NATIONAL SECURITY ACTION MEMORANDUM NO. 6
TO: The Director, Bureau of the Budget
SUBJECT: Plan of Reorganization of the Foreign Aid Program
This is merely a reminder from the National Security Council
meeting of February 1, 1961, that the Bureau of the Budget
has been charged by the President with the submission of
a plan of reorganization of the foreign aid program before
presentation to the Congress. This will not, of course, be
a surprise to the Bureau.
McGeorge Bundy
Special Assistant to the President
for National Security Affairs
Copy 3 of 4.
