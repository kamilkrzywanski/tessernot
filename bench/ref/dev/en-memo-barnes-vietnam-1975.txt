NATIONAL SECURITY COUNCIL

November 11, 1975
MEMORANDUM FOR: BRENT SCOWCROFT
FROM: THOMAS J. BARNES
SUBJECT: U.S. Reciprocal Steps Towards
North Vietnam
State proposed to the Secretary November 8 some modest steps whereby
we could lessen our public hostility toward Vietnam, and reciprocate for
the recent release of the nine American prisoners. State suggested five
options and Assistant Secretary Habib recommended the first two. Ken
Quinn's November 11 memo to me (Tab A) summarizes the options.
