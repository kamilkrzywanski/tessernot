OGE FORM 202, PART 1
NOTIFICATION OF CONFLICT OF INTEREST REFERRAL
PART 1: INITIAL NOTIFICATION
(to be filed by the referring office)
File Part 1 of this form with the U.S. Office of Government Ethics (OGE) upon any referral made to the
U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 535, involving:
(1) A possible violation under section 203, 205, 207, 208, and/or 209 of title 18, United States Code;
(2) A civil or criminal matter related to the filing or non-filing of a financial disclosure report under
applicable legal authorities (e.g., 5 U.S.C. § 13106 or 18 U.S.C. § 1001); or
(3) A civil matter involving outside earned income under 5 U.S.C. § 13143 or outside activities under 5
U.S.C. § 13144.
Submit this form via email to referrals@oge.gov. OGE will confirm receipt and notify the referring office of
the OGE tracking number assigned.
