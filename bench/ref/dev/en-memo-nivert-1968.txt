SUBJECT: Virginia's ratification of the 19th Amendment
In February we received a call from Senator Spong's office asking if
Virginia had ratified the 19th Amendment. We were told that one of
the state legislators wanted to petition the Congress to recognize
the fact of ratification sometime in 1952.
We checked the Constitutional amendment files and the legislative files
in the National Archives, but found no record of ratification. We told
this to Mr. Maeder of the Senators' office. We also explained the
Federal procedures for ratifications.
As a result, the Administrator received House Joint Resolution No. 75,
which petitioned the Congress to recognize Virginia's ratification of
February 21, 1952.
After receiving the Resolution from the Administrators office, I called
the office of the Clerk of the House of Virginia and talked to Miss Cocke,
Deputy Clerk. I explained the role of the General Services Administra-
tion in the Amendment process. I asked her for a certified copy of the
resolution of ratification. She sent a copy of House Joint Resolution
No. 44.
These documents together with phone calls to Miss Cocke verified
Virginia's ratification of the 19th Amendment on February 21, 1952.
Virginia had previously rejected the proposed amendment on February 12,
1920.
I called the Law Revision Counsel of the House Judiciary Committee
(Charles Zinn) and the Senate Rules Committee (John Coder). This
information will be added to "The Constitution of the United States"
issued by the House and to the Senate Manual.
