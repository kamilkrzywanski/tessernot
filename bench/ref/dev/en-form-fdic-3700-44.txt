FDIC LEASING REPRESENTATIONS AND CERTIFICATIONS
PART I – CERTIFICATIONS CONCERNING THE PREMISES, THE BUILDING AND THE LAND
NOTE: The foregoing Instructions describe what a Lessor must provide if a “False” box is checked regarding any of the following
statements.
1. Compliance with Laws
a. The Premises, the Building and the Land are in compliance with all existing health, safety, fire, zoning, building, and
environmental laws, rules, regulations, codes and ordinances applicable to the Premises, the Building or the Land,
respectively (excluding, for this Question #1(a), the Americans with Disabilities Act, 42 U.S.C. 12101 et. seq.).
True False
b. The Building and the Land (excluding areas occupied by other tenants, but including, without limitation, common areas,
existing bathroom facilities (in the Premises or in public areas), paths of travel to and from the Premises and ingress/egress
Building access points (including, if applicable, any parking facility being provided by Lessor), elevators and drinking fountains)
are in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, 42 U.S.C. 12101 et. seq., and any similar state or local law, rule,
regulation, code or ordinance applicable to the Building or the Land.
True False
c. There is no “grandfather” provision relating to the Premises, the Building or the Land that results in the Premises, the
Building or the Land, as applicable, being in compliance with the requirements described in Question #1(a) or Question
#1(b) above such that, if that “grandfather” provision did not exist, the Premises, the Building or the Land otherwise would
not be in compliance with any such requirement.
True False
2. Asbestos-Containing Materials.
a. The Premises and/or the Building have been assessed for the presence of Asbestos-Containing Materials (defined
below).
True False
If “True,” attach the most recent Asbestos-Containing Material assessment report for the Premises/Building.
b. The Premises and the Building do not contain any Asbestos-Containing Material.
True False
The term “Asbestos-Containing Material” means any material with a concentration of one percent (1%) or greater by dry weight
of asbestos.
3. Water Quality.
a. The quality of the water at the Premises and/or the Building has been assessed.
True False
If “True,” attach the most recent water quality assessment report for the Premises/Building.
b. The water at all drinking fountains, sinks or other sources of water at the Premises and the Building is potable and does
not contain lead, copper or other contaminants in excess of maximum contaminant levels established by the United States
Environmental Protection Agency and applicable state and municipal drinking water standards.
True False
