Nancy Pelosi

January 23, 2019
The President
The White House
1600 Pennsylvania Avenue, Northwest

Dear Mr. President:
When I extended an invitation on January 3ʳᵈ for you to deliver the State of the Union address, it
was on the mutually agreed upon date, January 29ᵗʰ.  At that time, there was no thought that the
government would still be shut down.
In my further correspondence of January 16ᵗʰ, I said we should work together to find a mutually
agreeable date when government has re-opened and I hope that we can still do that.
I am writing to inform you that the House of Representatives will not consider a concurrent
resolution authorizing the President’s State of the Union address in the House Chamber until
government has opened.
Again, I look forward to welcoming you to the House on a mutually agreeable date for this address
when government has been opened.
Sincerely,
NANCY PELOSI
Speaker of the House
