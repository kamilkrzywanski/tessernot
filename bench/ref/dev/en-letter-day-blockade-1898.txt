DEPARTMENT OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.
July 2, 1898.
The Honorable
The Secretary of the Navy.
Sir:-
I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt of your
letter of the 28th ultimo, enclosing copy of your telegram
to the Commander-in-Chief of the United States Naval Forces
off Santiago de Cuba, announcing the issue of the President's
proclamation of a blockade of the Southern Coast of Cuba
and of the port of San Juan de Puerto Rico.
Respectfully yours,
