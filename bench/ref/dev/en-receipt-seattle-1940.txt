DEPARTMENT OF POLICE—TRAFFIC DIVISION
A Friendly Warning LICENSE NO.
Your Car Is Parked Improperly
When parking, if headed uphill, turn your steering wheel to the left and allow
the front wheels to back gently at a sharp angle to the curb. Then set your hand
brake and put the car in low gear.
If you are headed downhill, turn your steering wheel to the right and allow the
front wheels to move forward at a sharp angle to the curb. Then set your hand brake
and put the car in reverse gear.
Do not depend entirely upon your hand brake to hold the car. The cooling brake
drum may expand and allow the brake to loosen, thus causing the car to run away.
THIS WARNING IS GIVEN YOU IN THE INTEREST OF SAFETY TO YOUR
CAR AND TO THE LIVES AND PROPERTY OF OTHERS.
W. H. SEARS, Chief of Police.
CAPT. JOSEPH E. PRINCE,
A FAIR REQUEST–BE CAREFUL Commanding Traffic Division.
