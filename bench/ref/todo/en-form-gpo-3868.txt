NOTIFICATION OF INTENT TO PUBLISH — Form 3868
To ensure that your publication will receive proper consideration for public distribution programs, please
supply as much of the following information as possible. GPO’s Federal Depository Library Program
(FDLP) distributes publications to the nation’s libraries, making them more widely available to the public.
Agencies printing external to GPO are required to provide between 1 - 1000 copies of publications,
depending on content, for distribution. Distribution is provided at no cost to the Agency. Another
publication distribution outlet you may want to consider utilizing is GPO Sales. Please submit this form
up to 30 days before sending your Printing Requisition to learn how many additional copies
are needed. This form itself is not a printing requisition.
