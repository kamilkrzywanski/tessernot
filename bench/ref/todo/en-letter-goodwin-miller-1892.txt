H. STANLEY GOODWIN,

Bangor, Pa.
Dear Sir:-
I send you herewith map showing the Easton & Northern
R.R. with corrections made as per your suggestions. Will you
kindly return this to me with the distances between stations
marked on it? I will then have some more blue prints made and will
send you some copies.
Yours truly,
General Eastern Supt.
