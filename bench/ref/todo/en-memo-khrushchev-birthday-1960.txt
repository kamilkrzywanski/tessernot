MEMORANDUM FOR THE PRESIDENT
SUBJECT: BIRTHDAY MESSAGE TO KHRUSHCHEV
KHRUSHCHEV'S SIXTY-SIXTH BIRTHDAY OCCURS APRIL 17.
YOU WILL RECALL THAT LAST OCTOBER KHRUSHCHEV SENT YOU
BIRTHDAY GREETINGS TO WHICH YOU REPLIED. WITH THE FORTHCOMING
SUMMIT MEETING AND YOUR TRIP TO THE SOVIET UNION IN MIND,
I RECOMMEND THAT YOU SEND A BRIEF BIRTHDAY MESSAGE TO KHRUSHCHEV.
I AM ENCLOSING A DRAFT OF SUCH A MESSAGE WHICH, IF YOU APPROVE,
COULD BE TELEGRAPHED TO OUR EMBASSY AT MOSCOW FOR DELIVERY.
SGD LIVINGSTON . MERCHANT
LIVINGSTON T. MERCHANT
ACTING SECRETARY
