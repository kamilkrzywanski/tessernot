Dear Republican Colleague:
This is a staggering day for our Conference. We are all losing colleagues and friends
who’ve been valuable members of our Conference and leaders in our party. As we look to the
future, we must learn from these results.
In times like these, I’m reminded of the resilience and optimism of the American people.
When Americans get knocked down, they pick themselves up and get back to work.
Now our Conference must do the same.
In these next two years, our job cannot be merely to spotlight how radical this new
Democrat party is—offense, not defense, wins elections. Republican policies prioritize making
Americans’ neighborhoods safer, keeping more money in their pockets, and protecting enduring
American values and freedoms. To restore our majority, we must restore voters’ faith in our
ability to deliver on these principles.
That is why I am running to serve as Republican Leader in the 120th Congress.
For too long, we have operated without a clear plan to survive the hour or the day in front
of us. That will not cut it against an emboldened and energetic Democrat majority.
We can expect Democrat legislation to wreak havoc on every sector of our economy and
transform time-tested American institutions. Meanwhile, Democrats have already signaled their
true intentions. Each Democrat-led committee will turn their full attention to investigating every
facet of the Administration and private entities across the country.
I am battle-tested on the oversight front and I know the Democrats’ playbook. I know
what it will take to lead our Conference in the minority, to stay unified, and to coordinate and
calibrate our response to the Democrats’ tactics. We need a Leadership team that is active and
engaged, forward-thinking and straight-shooting.
Communication will be key—both among our Conference and with the American people.
We need close coordination in an open, honest, and regular manner. To our constituents, we must
present clear alternatives to the progressive policies of the Democrats. Direct communication
