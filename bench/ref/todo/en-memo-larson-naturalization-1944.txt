E&RAU February 25, 1944
In re: ANDREAS JOHAN LARSON
The above named 38 year old married male, native
and citizen of Norway, Scandinavian race, filed an applica-
tion executed December 6, 1943 for the benefits of Section
308 of the Nationality Act of 1940, to cover his absence
from the United States from October 23, 1937 to October 15,
1942, while engaged abroad as a missionary by the Colombia
Evangelical Lutheran Mission of South America.
The record discloses that the applicant was ad-
mitted to the United States for permanent residence at the
port of New York on April 26, 1929. He has submitted
satisfactory evidence to establish that he is the spouse
of a citizen of the United States, and therefore eligible
to proceed toward naturalization without first filing a
declaration of intention.
The Secretary of the Colombia Evangelical Lutheran
Mission of South America certified on February 7, 1944 that
the applicant, an ordained Minister of the Gospel was
engaged solely in missionary work in South America from
October 23, 1937 to October 15, 1942.
The evidence presented satisfactorily establishes
that the subject was absent from this country in a capacity
as a regularly ordained clergyman, and therefore his applica-
tion may be properly approved.
IT IS ORDERED that Andreas Johan Larson be granted
the benefits of Section 308 of the Nationality Act of 1940
to cover his absence from the United States from October 23,
1937 to October 15, 1942, during which period he was engaged
abroad in duties of a regularly ordained clergyman in behalf
of the Colombia Evangelical Lutheran Mission of South
America, with the understanding that he shall prove to the
satisfaction of the court wherein his admission to United
States citizenship is proposed that he was absent during
that period for that purpose.
