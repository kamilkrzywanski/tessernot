August 21, 1961
NATIONAL SECURITY ACTION MEMORANDUM NO. 78
TO: Secretary of Defense
(SUBJECT: Berlin)
1. I wonder if we could get a report by the end of August
on what military build up in conventional forces we are going to
get from the NATO countries for the Berlin crisis. If you are
not satisfied with it should you go to Europe and meet with the
Defense Ministers of the countries involved. Or what other
steps would you suggest taking?
2. I think we should have a review of our contingency
plan for Berlin and also see whether we have reached any under-
standing with the governments for the implementation of these
plans. We have to assume that some time in the fall there may
be a blockade of a formal or informal kind. Have we decided
with the British and the French and the Germans what our response
will be?
3. If the Communists blockade West Berlin and we
begin an airlift should we not be reaching some decision as to
what other steps we would take against them besides economic.
Could we expect our Navy to carry out any blockade of the Soviet
Union for example in the Eastern Mediterranean or in the Northern
seas.
4. Would you talk to me about these matters at your
convenience.
/s/ John F. Kennedy
