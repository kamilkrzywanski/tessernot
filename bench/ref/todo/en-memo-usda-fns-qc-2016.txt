SUBJECT: Process for Measurement of the Fiscal Year 2016 Payment Error
Rate Quality Control Policy Memo: 17-02
TO: Regional Directors
Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program
All Regions
The Quality Control (QC) system is an integral part of the Food and Nutrition
Service’s (FNS) responsibility for oversight of the Supplemental Nutrition
Assistance Program (SNAP). After discovering data quality issues around
payment accuracy data submitted during the fiscal year (FY) 2015 and FY 2016
review periods, FNS took steps to ensure that future payment accuracy rates are
measured with the utmost integrity, including revising the FNS Handbook 310 to
clarify and expand requirements aimed at strengthening the QC system. FNS
also provided training on the revised handbook for all Federal and State QC staff.
These efforts are being fully implemented across States during the FY 2017
review period.
As part of the initiative to ensure the accuracy of the QC measure, and as noted
in the June 24, 2016, letter from Under Secretary Concannon, FNS will
undertake a single-tier review of FY 2016 cases, nationwide, in order to calculate
a National SNAP error rate for FY 2016. This informational memorandum
details the FY 2016 review process and outlines how FNS and the States will
work together in partnership to accomplish the tasks associated with a single-tier
review.
Sampling
In October 2016, FNS requested that States provide information on households
that received SNAP in September 2016. An FNS team, comprised of staff
statisticians and statisticians from our contractor, Accenture, is preparing a
National sample of 440 cases based on the information submitted by the 53 State
agencies. We expect the National sample to be complete in the first week of
January 2017. We expect to notify State agencies of any cases from their State
that are in the National QC sample by January 13, 2017 via the SNAP State
Quality Control System (QCS) contacts.
Providing Case Data
The complete certification and issuance files for cases included in the National
sample must be submitted by States to FNS via SNAP QCS by January 27, 2017.
This is similar to the existing process for uploading case information for cases
that are part of the Federal sub-sample in the two-tiered review system. Each
State is also asked to designate one contact person and two backup contacts to
provide additional case information, such as data match results, during the
review.
