Dear Secretary Wiedefeld:
Thank you for your letter of April 19, 2024, requesting the designation of the 18.8-mile segment
of Maryland State Route 695 from the northern interchange of I-95/MD-695 near Rossville,
Maryland, running clockwise and terminating at the interchange of I-97/MD-695 as I-695. Our
Maryland Division Office has approved the design exceptions related to the segment and
confirms that the segment meets the standards of the Interstate Highway System.
The segment is a logical addition to the Interstate System. I find the request meets the
requirements for addition to the Interstate System under Title 23, United States Code, Section
103(c)(4)(A). Based on our reviews and the American Association of State Highway and
Transportation Officials’ (AASHTO) approval of the numbering, I hereby approve the addition
of this segment to the Interstate System.
