Dear Colleague,
Reflecting on the last ten months of our House Republican Majority, our Conference has shown
what we can accomplish when we come together as a team. We repeatedly beat the odds to
pass signature pieces of our agenda on behalf of the American people because we came
together despite our razor-thin majority and made sure everyone had a seat at the table.
Thanks to Joe Biden and Democrats in Congress, Americans continue to experience an
untenable debt and spending crisis, an open border that creates a growing national security and
humanitarian crisis each day, an economy that is crushing families and Main Street, and weak
foreign policy that has enabled terrorism and done little to reassure our allies. We are in
uncharted waters, and our country faces serious challenges that the American people sent us to
Washington to solve.
In January, I stood with Speaker McCarthy because of his willingness to change the way this
institution operates. Over the last two weeks, I stood with Leader Scalise and Chairman Jordan
because they stepped into the void with the intent and vision to lead and unite us. Though we
must now look for a new path forward, their leadership in our Conference remains invaluable.
Our Conference remains at a crossroads and the deck is stacked against us. We have no
choice but to fight like hell to hold on to our House Majority and deliver on our conservative
agenda. Having had the privilege of getting to know all of you as your NRCC Chair and as your
Majority Whip, I know we are still ready for that fight.
At the NRCC, we fought side by side and worked for every inch of this majority in districts
across the country. We won seats for Republicans in back-to-back elections by creating a
culture of teamwork, communication, and respect. As your Majority Whip, we showed just how
effective we can be when members from across the Conference come together and apply that
same culture of teamwork, communication, and respect.
What are the results? The most significant border security legislation since 1996, one that was
built with the input from - and supported by - members from every corner of our Conference. A
revived appropriations process not seen in more than a decade. And the first Republican-only
NDAA passed in history, fighting against a woke Defense Department agenda while investing in
our troops and our strategic capabilities.
Each week has brought new challenges. But delivering historic change should not be easy.
If given the opportunity to be your Speaker, we will use that same culture of teamwork,
communication, and respect to build on the moments that brought us success, learn from our
mistakes, and keep fighting for each and every one of you and our Republican majority.
I will also always be honest and direct with all of you, even if we disagree. I will never make a
promise I cannot fulfill. I expect to be held accountable, and you can expect that we will also
keep you to your word.
Our constituents who sent us all to Washington to lead are counting on us to undo the damage
Democrats have done and expand our conservative majority. We must come together for the
good of the American people and ensure that our best days are ahead. With that, I humbly ask
for your support for Speaker of the House and look forward to discussing this in more detail with
you in the coming days as we continue our mission to correct the course of our great nation.
Sincerely,
