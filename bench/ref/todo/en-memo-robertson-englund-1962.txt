SUBJECT: R. T. Englund Company
At Mr. Misler's suggestion, and at the request of Mrs. Katherine Peake, who met
with Millard Cass in Washington on August 9, 1962, I reached Mrs. Peake by
telephone in Los Angeles on October 2. I advised Mrs. Peake of the general status
of the investigation of the Department in the R. T. Englund Company matter and
urged her not to give any premature publicity to the matter because to do so would
militate against the investigation also being made with respect to seven other
lettuce growers (whom I did not identify), which investigations were not yet
completed. I advised her that some of those investigations could not be resumed
until after October 15 when certain Cooler records would become available.
Mrs. Peake advised me that a great deal of pressure was being generated within the
labor movement for an early disclosure of the situation involving R. T. Englund
and other Imperial Valley lettuce growers. She indicated that this pressure was
reaching very close to George Meany's office. She also advised me very frankly
that she and her group were interested in having this issue broken before
November 6. She indicated that the last week of October would be the very latest
date that they would expect some announcement concerning this matter. I told
Mrs. Peake I would pass this information on to the Department in Washington. I
also extracted a promise from her that she would call me before any publicity
was broken outside the Department of Labor.
Mrs. Peake can be reached in Los Angeles from October 3 through October 5 at the
office of Max Mont, head of the Jewish Labor Committee, telephone Normandy 2-1148.
Her telephone number in Santa Barbara is Woodland 9-2855.
