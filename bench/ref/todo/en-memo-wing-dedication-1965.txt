Memorandum
To: Regional Director, Northeast Region
From: Superintendent, Gettysburg
Subject: Dedication Ceremony for UDC Memorial
On August 25, the United Daughters of the Confederacy will be
dedicating the memorial designed by Mr. DeLue, at the site on South
Confederate Avenue. Sen. Sam Ervin will be the principal speaker;
it is expected that several governors will be present; it promises
to be an important affair.
One immediate responsibility for the Park is to arrange a speaker's
stand, seating for audience, and other physical details. My first
thought is to secure (again) the portable stand belonging to NCR,
and probably a few hundred folding chairs. Our men and trucks can
make the pick-up and return of this equipment.
If you endorse this method of handling the arrangements, please
place the request with NCR, or authorize direct contact with NCR
by this office.
Kittridge A. Wing
