PRIVACY ACT STATEMENT
Authority: The Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, as amended; the Act of August 26, 1950; and Executive Order 10450: Security requirements for
Government Employment constitute authority for collecting the information.
Purpose: The purpose of this form is to collect information to allow for the completion of the individual’s background investigation minimizing the need
for extensive foreign inquiries. This form is to be completed by any person having had overseas residence within the time limitation specified on the
form. The ability to verify foreign activities through references who are in the United States significantly shortens the timetable for an investigation.
Routine Uses: The intent is to obtain the names, locations and means to contact individuals in the United States who can verify foreign activities, i.e.,
employments or unemployment periods, and residences. Information collected may be used by USAID for routine uses permitted under system of
records notice USAID-008, Personnel Security and Suitability Investigation Records, 78 FR 25414 (October 29, 2013). Disclosure of the information
provided will not be made outside the Agency without written consent except (a) pursuant to applicable routine use listed under System of Records
Notice USAID-008 Personnel Security and Suitability Investigations Records, or (b) when disclosure without the candidate’s consent is authorized by
the Privacy Act, 5 USC 552a and provided in 22 CFR 215, Regulations for Implementation of Privacy Act of 1974. (A copy of the Regulation and
Notice of Systems of Records is available from the Information and Records Division on request.)
Disclosure: Disclosure of personal information is voluntary. However, failure to provide the requested personal information may result in an individual
not being eligible for employment with USAID and/or for completion of security clearance, update and/or upgrade actions.
