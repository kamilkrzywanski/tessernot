The Honorable Robert F. Kennedy
Attorney General of the United States
Washington 25, D. C.
Dear Bobby:
The President wrote a book on immigration entitled "A Nation of
Immigrants" which was released by the Anti-Defamation League while
he was in the Senate. Last summer the President, along with Mike
Feldman, was rewriting this book. It was not quite completed, however,
in November.
At the present time there remains three alternatives in handling the
publication of the book.
(1) The Anti-Defamation League is going to reprint it as
it was originally.
(2) It could be completed by Mike Feldman, published and
released by the League.
(3) Released by the Anti-Defamation League as originally
written with modifications by the President.
Mike Feldman is uncertain as to what course to follow. The League
has been pressing him for an answer. Could you talk to him and let
me know what is decided. The Boston Herald would like to run it as
an exclusive series, donating the proceeds to the Library.
Best wishes.
Sincerely,
Edward M. Kennedy
