SHROPSHIRE.

FREEHOLD ESTATE,

The Coalbrookdale Company, Limited,

900 ACRES.

Several conveniently arranged FARMS,

LAWLEY FARM, HORSEHAY FARM, LAWLEY BANK FARM, and NEW DALE FARM.
MANY SUPERIOR
VILLA RESIDENCES,
With GROUNDS and GARDENS.
Several SHOPS and PREMISES.
336 Good Cottages,
WITH
LARGE GARDENS.
MANY LOTS OF
Accommodation Land and Garden Ground.
BUILDING SITES.
The Pool Hill County Council Schools.
The Ground Rents of Three Chapels,
ALSO
Horsehay & Heathill Coppices,
HORSEHAY POOL & CINDER HILL,
Also all the Existing Leases and Contracts of the Mines and Minerals on and under the above.
